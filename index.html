Building a Trustworthy Campus

Our platform is designed with security and community integrity at its core.




Verified Reports


All found item reports are manually screened by campus staff to ensure accuracy and prevent spam.


Secure Process


Strict identity verification is required before any item is released to a claimant.


Instant Notifications


Get real-time alerts when an item matching your lost report is turned into our system.


Staff Support


Dedicated campus officers available 8am-6pm to assist with complex retrieval situations.

Don't Lose it Again

Label Everything

Use permanent marker or stickers on laptops, water bottles, and chargers.

Use Digital Trackers

Attach AirTags or similar Bluetooth trackers to your keys and bags.

Find My... Active

Ensure 'Find My iPhone' or 'Google Find My Device' is always enabled.

Take an Inventory

Keep a photo of your most valuable items and their serial numbers.

Buddy Check

Remind friends to check their seats before leaving a common area.

Lock Screen Contact

Set a wallpaper with your emergency contact info on your phone/tablet.
