💡 Tips for a Better Report

  • Be as specific as possible — brand, colour, model number
  • Mention any unique scratches, stickers, or engravings
  • Report as soon as you notice the item is missing
  • Double-check the last location you were at on campus
  • Provide a reliable contact so we can reach you fast

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do you keep lost items? ▾
All found items are kept for a minimum of 30 days. High-value items such as electronics, ID cards, and wallets may be kept longer. After the holding period, unclaimed items are donated to campus charities.
What do I need to collect my item? ▾
Bring a valid student or staff ID to the office. You may also be asked to describe the item in detail or show proof of ownership (e.g. a photo on your phone, a receipt).
Can someone else collect on my behalf? ▾
Yes — they must bring a signed authorisation letter from you, their own ID, and a copy of your student/staff ID.
What if my item isn't listed on this site? ▾
New items are added daily. You can also call or email us directly and one of our staff will physically check the storage room for you.
